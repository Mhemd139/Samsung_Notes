Get your notes out of Samsung Notes

  1. In Samsung Notes, long-press a note, then select all the notes you want.
  2. Tap Share, then Samsung Notes file.
  3. Save them to your files or cloud drive, then open them here. On Android, install this app to share them straight to it.

Questions

Is it really private?

Yes. Inkport runs entirely in your browser. Your files are read on your device and the page can't send them anywhere: its security policy blocks it, and the code is open source.

Which files can it open?

Samsung Notes files (.sdocx), a folder of them, or a .zip. Older formats such as .sdoc and .snb aren't supported.

Can Claude read my whole library?

Yes. On a computer, the free Samsung Notes connector lets Claude read every note directly. Set up the connector
